# 15. ui.library: gadgets in a window

*Written, V8 step 6.*

ui.library puts controls in a program's window: toggles, sliders, a mode selector, buttons, labels, a list, a text field, and the panes and grooves they sit on, in §13.2's colours ([docs §13.3](../../docs/13-workbench.md#widget-toolkit-uilibrary)). It lays them out, draws them into the window's buffer with the program's gfx.library (chapter 12), and turns the window's IDCMP events (chapter 13) into changes: which gadget, and its new value.

It's a shared library (chapter 8), reached through `IUI`. Its functions are in `planar/ui.h` and `lib/ui`. It keeps nothing of its own. Everything is in the caller's `struct UI`, and a gadget is the program's pixels: intuition knows nothing of it.

## Concepts

- **A `struct UI`** is the caller's, about 6 KiB: the gadgets, the fonts, which gadget has the focus and which is pressed, and the damage since the window was last presented. [InitUI](../autodocs/ui.md#uiinitui) makes it ready and opens System Sans and System Mono through the caller's `IGfx`; [FreeUI](../autodocs/ui.md#uifreeui) closes them.
- **Gadgets** are a tree of up to `UI_GADGETS` (48), numbered from 1 as they're added. The first is the root, over the whole inside. **Groups** hold the others: a row, a column, or a split pane of two. The rest are leaves:

| Kind | What it is | Its value |
|---|---|---|
| `UI_SPLITPANE` | a dark pane and a light one, a 2 px groove between, dragged | the left's width |
| `UI_DIVIDER` | a 2 px etched groove across | — |
| `UI_TOGGLE` | a 52×22 pill switch: cobalt, or amber with a glyph on its track; muted when disabled | 0 or 1 |
| `UI_SLIDER` | a 6 px track, filled cobalt to a silver knob | `UIA_Min` to `UIA_Max` |
| `UI_SEGMENTED` | a capsule of options, the chosen one amber | the option |
| `UI_HUDBADGE` | a dark card of System Mono lines, right-aligned | — |
| `UI_BUTTON` | a silver button, amber while pressed | 1 when clicked |
| `UI_LABEL` | text, left, centred or right | — |
| `UI_LIST` | rows of 22 px in a well, the selected one cobalt | the row, -1 none |
| `UI_TEXTFIELD` | a line of text in a well, edited | its length, on Return |

- **Panes.** Everything's drawn on a pane's gradient: a split pane's left side dark slate, its right side silver, and the root silver unless it says otherwise (`UIA_Pane`). Labels are light with a shadow on the dark pane, and dark with an etch on the light one.
- **The focus** is the gadget keys go to, ringed in amber. A press on a toggle, slider, selector, button, list or text field gives it the focus, and Tab moves it.
- **Damage.** Whatever ui.library draws, it adds to `ui_Damage`: up to 16 rectangles, then one around them all. The caller presents them ([PresentWindow](../autodocs/intuition.md#intuitionpresentwindow)) and sets `ui_NumDamage` to 0.

## Building a window's gadgets

Gadgets are added with [AddGadget](../autodocs/ui.md#uiaddgadget), each to a group, with tags. From `conformance/uiwin.h`, which `conformance/window.c` opens on QEMU and `lib/ui/test.c` draws on the host:

```c
AddGadgetTags(IUI, ui, 0, UI_SPLITPANE, { UIA_Value, 240 });
AddGadgetTags(IUI, ui, UW_SPLIT, UI_COLUMN, { UIA_Margin, 14 }, { UIA_Spacing, 10 });
AddGadgetTags(IUI, ui, UW_LEFTCOL, UI_TOGGLE, { UIA_Label, (uint64_t)"Key repeat" });
AddGadgetTags(IUI, ui, UW_LEFTCOL, UI_SLIDER, { UIA_Label, (uint64_t)"Speed" }, { UIA_Min, 0 },
              { UIA_Max, 20 }, { UIA_Value, 5 });
...
AddGadgetTags(IUI, ui, UW_RIGHTCOL, UI_LIST, { UIA_Items, (uint64_t)uw_files }, { UIA_Count, 8 },
              { UIA_Weight, 1 });
AddGadgetTags(IUI, ui, UW_RIGHTCOL, UI_TEXTFIELD, { UIA_Buffer, (uint64_t)field }, { UIA_Size, UW_FIELD });
AddGadgetTags(IUI, ui, UW_RIGHTCOL, UI_ROW, { UIA_Align, UIALIGN_END });
```

A gadget's strings, items and buffer stay the program's, and must outlive it. [SetGadgetAttrs](../autodocs/ui.md#uisetgadgetattrsa) changes one later: a value the program sets itself, a gadget disabled.

## Layout

[LayoutUI](../autodocs/ui.md#uilayoutui) measures every gadget and places it, for the window's inside's size. Each gadget has its own size, from §13.3's sizes and its text. A row adds its children's widths and a column their heights, with `UIA_Spacing` between and `UIA_Margin` around. What's left over is shared by `UIA_Weight` among the children that have one; with none, it's left at the end. Across a group, a child fills it (a column's default), or sits at its start, middle (a row's default) or end. In the example, the list's weight gives it the column's height left over, and the buttons' row sits at the column's right.

Call it at the start and again at `IDCMP_NEWSIZE`, with the new size, before [DrawUI](../autodocs/ui.md#uidrawui).

## The event loop

```c
ui.ui_RPort = &w.w_RPort;
IUI->LayoutUI(&ui, w.w_Width, w.w_Height);
IUI->DrawUI(&ui);
IIntuition->PresentWindow(&w, nullptr, 0);
ui.ui_NumDamage = 0;

while (IIntuition->WaitIntuiEvent(&in, 0) == 0)
    while (IIntuition->GetIntuiEvent(&in, &ev)) {
        IIntuition->WaitSafe(&w, deadline);         /* one buffer: drawn once intuition's done with it */
        if (IUI->HandleUIEvent(&ui, &ev, &c) == SPEED)
            ...                                     /* c.uc_Value, at once */
        if (ui.ui_NumDamage)
            IIntuition->PresentWindow(&w, ui.ui_Damage, ui.ui_NumDamage);
        ui.ui_NumDamage = 0;
    }
```

The window asks for `IDCMP_MOUSEBUTTONS`, `IDCMP_MOUSEMOVE` (for a drag), `IDCMP_VANILLAKEY` and `IDCMP_RAWKEY`. [HandleUIEvent](../autodocs/ui.md#uihandleuievent) says what changed as it happens: a slider at every step of a drag (`UICF_DRAG`) and once more when it's let go of, a list's double click (`UICF_DOUBLE`), a text field's Return (`UICF_ENTER`). Prefs sets a server's setting on each.

## Rules

- The UI, its strings and its buffers are the program's. ui.library holds no pointer of its own and allocates nothing but the fonts.
- Set `ui_RPort` before drawing, and again if the window's buffers change.
- Present the damage after each event, and zero `ui_NumDamage`, or the next event's adds to it.
- With one buffer, wait for it to be safe before an event that may draw.
- Lay out again at `IDCMP_NEWSIZE`. Changing a label or a list's items doesn't lay out anything.
- One task at a time uses a UI.

## Differences from the RKM

- **Retained, not intuition's.** The RKM's gadgets were intuition's: it hit-tested and drew them, and `GadTools` made them. Here they're the program's pixels, hit-tested and drawn in its own domain, and intuition knows only that the window was clicked. A crash in a gadget is the program's.
- **Layout is the library's.** GadTools gadgets were placed by the program, by pixel. ui.library lays out rows and columns, as later toolkits (MUI, ReAction) did on the Amiga.
- **One value a gadget.** `IDCMP_GADGETUP`'s `IAddress` and `Code` become `HandleUIEvent`'s `UIChange`.
- **No menus, tabs or trees** (decision 8): menus are intuition's (chapter 13).
- **Opaque.** The HUD badge is solid `#232C40`, not §13.3's 80%, since V8 has no translucency (decision 11).
